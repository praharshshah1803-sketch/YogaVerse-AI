# Business Owner Interview Script — YogaVerse AI

## Objective
Understand how Yoga By Rachna Shah currently acquires, serves, onboards, and retains students and instructors, and where founder capacity limits growth.

## Questions
1. Walk me through how a new student finds Yoga By Rachna Shah today.
2. What questions do prospective students ask before they commit?
3. Which parts of onboarding or follow-up take the most of your time?
4. Which tasks do you repeat every week?
5. If you added five instructors next month, what would break first?
6. How would you decide whether an instructor is good enough to represent the brand?
7. What information do you wish you had about student demand, conversion, and retention?
8. Which services or programs are easiest to sell today?
9. What makes students stay? What makes them leave?
10. What would you never want AI to do on behalf of the brand?
11. What would make you trust an AI-generated instructor profile or student recommendation?
12. What would you pay for or invest in if the product clearly saved time or generated revenue?

## Evidence to capture
- Current manual steps.
- Time spent per week.
- Current tools/channels.
- Examples of recent student/instructor cases.
- Current pricing/subscription practices.
- Decisions that require Rachna's personal judgment.
