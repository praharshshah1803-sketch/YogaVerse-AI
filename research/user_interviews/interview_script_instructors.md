# Instructor Interview Script — YogaVerse AI

## Objective
Understand instructor operations, student management, content creation, technology comfort, and the value proposition for joining the Yoga By Rachna Shah brand.

## Questions
1. Walk me through a typical week of teaching and non-teaching work.
2. Which tasks take time away from teaching?
3. How do you currently manage enquiries, scheduling, follow-ups, and payments?
4. How do you create or update your online profile and marketing content?
5. Which students are easiest for you to serve well? Which are hardest to match?
6. What information do you need before accepting a new student?
7. What would you want an AI assistant to draft or automate?
8. What should always remain under your personal review?
9. How would you feel about joining a yoga brand where an admin approves your profile and content?
10. What would make you trust a student matching system?
11. Would you pay for software that generates leads or saves administrative time? Would revenue share be preferable?
12. What concerns would stop you from using an AI-enabled instructor platform?

## Evidence to capture
- Repetitive admin hours.
- Current tools.
- Content workload.
- Student acquisition method.
- Trust and control requirements.
- Pricing/revenue-share preferences.
