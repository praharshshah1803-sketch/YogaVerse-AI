# Student Interview Script — YogaVerse AI

## Objective
Understand how students discover yoga, choose instructors/content, build consistency, and decide whether to pay for ongoing guidance.

## Questions
1. Tell me about the last time you tried to start or restart yoga.
2. Where did you look for a class, instructor, or video?
3. How did you decide what to try?
4. What was confusing or frustrating?
5. What made you continue or stop?
6. Do you prefer a fixed program, a live instructor, or self-paced videos? Why?
7. What would make you trust a personalized recommendation?
8. Would you share your goals, schedule, and general wellness information to receive a personalized starting plan?
9. What would make an AI recommendation feel helpful rather than generic?
10. What would make you uncomfortable about an AI wellness coach?
11. Would you consider paying monthly for personalized yoga plans, content, and/or instructor support?
12. What price range feels reasonable for something you would actually use?

## Evidence to capture
- Discovery channels.
- Decision criteria.
- Main barrier to consistency.
- Trust concerns.
- Preferred support model.
- Willingness to pay.
