# Product Vision — YogaVerse AI

## Vision
Build an AI-powered wellness platform that helps people discover the right yoga journey while enabling yoga instructors and wellness businesses to scale through intelligent automation.

## Mission
Make personalized yoga guidance easier to discover, easier to follow, and easier for trusted instructors to deliver at scale.

## Why Yoga By Rachna Shah is the pilot
YogaVerse AI is being designed around a real operating business: **Yoga By Rachna Shah**. The pilot gives the product a real environment to test onboarding, personalization, instructor capacity, lead conversion, and recurring revenue before attempting broader scale.

## Core product beliefs
1. **Wellness guidance, not medical diagnosis.** The product should help users form safe wellness habits and clearly escalate questions that require professional medical care.
2. **AI amplifies the instructor.** AI should reduce repetitive work and improve discovery while preserving instructor judgment, trust, and accountability.
3. **Brand-specific intelligence beats generic AI.** Recommendations should reflect the methodology, content, language, and boundaries of the business rather than behaving like a generic chatbot.
4. **Personalization must earn trust.** The product should explain why a recommendation was made and avoid collecting information that is not useful to the user's journey.

## 12-month pilot targets
- 300+ completed wellness assessments.
- 100+ monthly active practitioners completing at least one recommended session per week.
- 25+ instructor profiles onboarded or in the onboarding pipeline.
- 15%+ assessment-to-consultation conversion.
- 60%+ recommendation acceptance among users shown a recommendation.
- 4.2/5+ average user rating for AI recommendation quality.
- 0% target for safety-critical AI violations in reviewed outputs.

These are pilot targets, not current business results.

## What success looks like

### Students
A new user can explain their goal, get a relevant starting path, find a suitable instructor, and take a clear next action without browsing a large content library blindly.

### Instructors
An instructor can create a credible digital profile, package knowledge into programs, respond to routine questions faster, and spend more time teaching rather than doing repetitive admin.

### Business owner / admin
Rachna can onboard instructors under the Yoga By Rachna Shah brand, monitor demand and conversion, and increase capacity without every operational task depending on her personal time.

## What YogaVerse AI is not
- Not a medical diagnosis or treatment product.
- Not a generic AI chatbot with no business-specific knowledge.
- Not a gym chain or fitness aggregator.
- Not a replacement for qualified yoga instructors.
- Not a marketplace-first product in Phase 1; the initial focus is proving the pilot journey end to end.
