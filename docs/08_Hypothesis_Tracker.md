# Hypothesis Tracker

## HYP-001: Assessment improves conversion
**Status:** Untested  
**We believe:** Users who complete the AI wellness assessment are more likely to take a conversion action than users who only browse.  
**Test:** Compare consultation CTA conversion for assessment completers versus non-completers.  
**Success:** ≥1.5x conversion after 200 assessments.  
**Kill:** <1.2x after sample quality is sufficient.  
**Data needed:** assessment_start, assessment_complete, recommendation_view, CTA_click, consultation_booked.

## HYP-002: Personalization increases recommendation acceptance
**Status:** Untested  
**We believe:** Users are more likely to accept a recommendation when it explicitly reflects their goals and constraints.  
**Test:** Compare personalized recommendation cards with generic category cards.  
**Success:** ≥60% recommendation acceptance.  
**Kill:** <35% after 200 recommendation impressions.

## HYP-003: Explanation increases trust
**Status:** Untested  
**We believe:** Showing a short “why this was recommended” explanation improves user trust.  
**Test:** A/B test rationale shown vs not shown.  
**Success:** +0.4/5 trust rating without reducing clicks.  
**Kill:** No measurable lift after 100 responses per variant.

## HYP-004: Instructor matching improves decision confidence
**Status:** Untested  
**We believe:** A ranked instructor recommendation makes choosing an instructor easier than a directory alone.  
**Test:** Usability test with 10+ students.  
**Success:** ≥60% can choose an instructor and articulate a clear reason.  
**Kill:** Decision confidence does not improve.

## HYP-005: AI can reduce instructor profile creation effort
**Status:** Untested  
**We believe:** AI-assisted profile generation reduces the time an instructor needs to create a publishable profile.  
**Test:** Time manual profile creation versus AI-assisted draft + human editing.  
**Success:** ≥50% reduction in active creation time with admin quality approval.  
**Kill:** <25% reduction or unacceptable quality.

## HYP-006: AI content drafting reduces admin time
**Status:** Untested  
**We believe:** Drafting recurring social/WhatsApp/blog content with AI reduces instructor admin work without reducing authenticity.  
**Test:** One-week content diary with and without AI assistance.  
**Success:** ≥30% time saving and ≥4/5 quality rating by instructor.  
**Kill:** <15% time saving or quality <3.5/5.

## HYP-007: A subscription can support recurring value
**Status:** Untested  
**We believe:** A subset of students will pay monthly for personalized plans, content access, and ongoing support.  
**Test:** Pricing concept interviews followed by a landing-page pricing experiment.  
**Success:** ≥20% of qualified prospects express credible willingness to pay at a tested price.  
**Kill:** <10% across multiple price/value configurations.

## HYP-008: Personalization improves 30-day retention
**Status:** Untested  
**We believe:** A structured personalized journey produces better return behavior than unstructured content browsing.  
**Test:** Cohort retention comparison.  
**Success:** ≥1.5x 30-day retention.  
**Kill:** <1.2x after 100+ users per comparable cohort.

## HYP-009: Business analytics reduce founder workload
**Status:** Untested  
**We believe:** A simple dashboard of leads, conversion, instructors, and retention reduces the time Rachna spends manually assembling status updates.  
**Test:** Time-and-motion comparison for four weekly reporting cycles.  
**Success:** ≥50% reduction in reporting effort.  
**Kill:** <25% reduction or dashboard not trusted for decisions.
