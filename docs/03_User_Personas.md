# User Personas

These personas are working hypotheses for the Day 1 product foundation. They should be refined after interviews with real Yoga By Rachna Shah students, instructors, and the business owner.

## Priya — Student, Beginner
**Age:** 28  
**Occupation:** Software engineer  
**Location:** Bengaluru  
**Yoga experience:** Beginner

**Goals**
- Start a sustainable yoga routine.
- Improve sleep and reduce day-to-day stress.
- Find short sessions that fit a demanding workday.
- Avoid wasting time deciding what to watch.

**Frustrations**
- Too many videos and conflicting recommendations.
- Unsure what is appropriate for a beginner.
- Starts routines enthusiastically but loses consistency.
- Doesn't know which instructor to trust.

**Current behavior**
Primarily uses YouTube and social media. Saves videos but often chooses based on title or thumbnail rather than a structured plan.

**Quote**
> “I don't need thousands of yoga videos. I need someone to tell me where to start.”

**Key metrics**
Assessment completion, first-session completion, week-2 return, recommendation acceptance, consultation conversion.

## Arjun — Student, Intermediate
**Age:** 35  
**Occupation:** Banker  
**Location:** Mumbai  
**Yoga experience:** Intermediate; approximately two years

**Goals**
- Go deeper into his practice.
- Improve flexibility and manage recurring back discomfort through appropriate wellness practices.
- Find an instructor whose teaching style matches his needs.
- Build a routine he can sustain around work.

**Frustrations**
- Generic content feels too broad.
- Instructor profiles rarely explain who the instructor is best suited for.
- He spends time researching before committing.

**Current behavior**
Uses online classes occasionally and relies on referrals for instructor discovery.

**Quote**
> “I know enough to know that a generic routine isn't necessarily the right routine for me.”

**Key metrics**
Instructor match satisfaction, recommendation acceptance, repeat sessions, paid conversion, retention.

## Meera — Independent Yoga Instructor
**Age:** 42  
**Occupation:** Yoga instructor  
**Location:** Ahmedabad  
**Teaching experience:** 8+ years

**Goals**
- Increase students without increasing administrative overhead.
- Build a credible digital presence.
- Spend more time teaching and less time answering repetitive queries.
- Understand which programs attract and retain students.

**Frustrations**
- Manual follow-up and scheduling.
- Limited comfort with technology and marketing.
- Difficulty turning expertise into reusable digital products.
- Limited visibility into business performance.

**Current behavior**
Relies on WhatsApp, referrals, spreadsheets, and social media. Most customer knowledge lives in conversations rather than a structured system.

**Quote**
> “I know how to teach yoga. I don't want to become a full-time marketer and operations manager.”

**Key metrics**
Onboarding time, leads generated, lead-to-student conversion, active students, revenue, admin hours saved.

## Rachna — Business Owner / Admin
**Age:** 50  
**Occupation:** Founder, Yoga By Rachna Shah  
**Location:** Ahmedabad  
**Experience:** Experienced yoga and wellness practitioner / business owner

**Goals**
- Expand the brand without every student interaction depending on her personally.
- Onboard trusted instructors under the Yoga By Rachna Shah brand.
- Preserve teaching quality and brand philosophy as the team grows.
- Create predictable recurring revenue.
- Get visibility into demand, conversion, instructor performance, and retention.

**Frustrations**
- Founder bandwidth becomes the bottleneck as demand grows.
- Instructor quality and brand consistency need human oversight.
- Operational data can become fragmented across channels.
- Scaling through more instructors requires a repeatable onboarding process.

**Current behavior**
Personally oversees critical student and business decisions, supported by digital channels and manual coordination.

**Quote**
> “I want the brand to grow beyond my personal capacity without losing the trust that made it work.”

**Key metrics**
Founder hours per student, instructor onboarding time, active instructors, revenue per instructor, recurring revenue, student retention, brand-quality incidents.

## Persona prioritization

For Phase 1, **Priya** is the primary end-user persona because the assessment and recommendation loop needs a simple first-time journey. **Rachna** is the primary business stakeholder because she owns the pilot and decides whether the product creates operational value. Meera and Arjun become critical during instructor onboarding and matching validation.
