# Competitive Analysis

## Market thesis

The yoga/wellness AI market splits into two camps that do not overlap:

- **Camp 1: Student-facing AI yoga platforms** that personalize the consumer experience but give instructors zero tools.
- **Camp 2: Instructor/studio SaaS** that help teachers manage operations but offer no student-side AI personalization.

No product currently combines student AI + instructor SaaS + marketplace in one yoga-native platform. YogaVerse AI is positioned in that gap.

---

## Camp 1: Student-Facing AI Yoga Platforms

### Cult.fit
**Scale:** 700+ centres, 40+ cities, 130M+ app downloads, 1M+ MAUs, ₹1,720 Cr revenue FY26 (41% YoY growth), EBITDA positive, filing for ₹3,500-4,000 Cr IPO. Valued at ~₹12,600 Cr.

**What they do well:** Tiered subscriptions (Cultpass ELITE/PRO/HOME from ₹116 to ₹4,890/mo), AI Trainer for personalized gym workout plans, Energy Meter for calorie tracking, 1,200+ workouts, live streaming classes, D2C commerce (Cultsport brand), franchise model across India.

**Where they fall short for us:** Yoga is one of 6+ workout formats with zero depth in tradition, methodology, or style-based personalization. No AI wellness assessment. Instructors are employees, not empowered professionals. No brand-specific AI knowledge. No instructor-student matching intelligence.

**Our opportunity:** Cult.fit is a gym chain that added yoga as a workout category. YogaVerse AI is a yoga-native intelligence platform. They treat yoga like cardio with different poses.

### Nirvaana Yoga (India)
**What they do:** Personalized therapeutic yoga app. AI-powered Asana Sheets. Programs for PCOS, thyroid, stress, arthritis. 100+ validated real student journeys. Expert-led video library across styles and levels.

**Strength:** Real health-condition targeting with user validation. They have done the work of mapping yoga to specific wellness conditions.

**Gap:** No instructor tools, no marketplace, no SaaS layer. It is a content app, not a platform.

**What to steal:** Their health-condition targeting approach. Our wellness assessment should explicitly ask about conditions and map to yoga traditions that address them.

### Aayu Health (Mumbai, India)
**What they do:** Science-backed yoga therapy for chronic diseases. Partnered with S-VYASA university (initiators of International Yoga Day). Ministry of Ayush recognized. Programs for diabetes, hypertension, PCOS, depression, post-cancer care. Doctor consultations + yoga therapy + diet plans. Corporate wellness arm. ~3,300 monthly web visits.

**Strength:** Institutional legitimacy. Government-backed, university-partnered, real clinical research behind their programs.

**Gap:** No AI personalization engine, no instructor empowerment, no SaaS. Essentially a curated content library with doctor access.

**What to steal:** The institutional legitimacy play. Explore partnerships with yoga research institutions, certifications, or wellness boards for Yoga By Rachna Shah. Also steal the corporate wellness B2B angle for future revenue.

### Buba (Launching 2026)
**What they do:** Adaptive real-time yoga. Start with a check-in, get a practice, real-time interaction signals refine future sessions. Builds long-term personal trends over time.

**Strength:** The closest concept to our AI assessment idea. Real-time adaptive loop is the smartest consumer UX in this space.

**Gap:** Pre-launch, no instructor side, consumer app only.

**What to steal:** The adaptive session loop. Make our assessment a living system: after every session, a 30-second check-in refines the next recommendation. This is the single best feature idea from the competitive landscape.

**Threat level: Medium.** Closest competitor on the student side.

### YogAI / Smart Movements (Canada)
**What they do:** AI pose feedback via device camera using computer vision. Real-time alignment correction. Wearable sensor integration for heart rate, stress, muscle tone. 500+ downloads.

**Strength:** Technically impressive CV-based pose recognition.

**Gap:** A pose-correction tool, not a platform. No personalized wellness journey, no instructor tools. Very early stage.

**What to steal:** Computer vision pose feedback. Not for MVP, but flag as a Phase 3 feature. Kevin's MSc in AI/ML makes this credible on a resume.

### Yogi.live (Hong Kong)
**What they do:** Built by yoga master Yogananth Andiappan (20+ years). AI analysis of 1,000+ poses covering anatomy, modifications, and therapeutic applications. Deep domain expertise baked into AI.

**Strength:** Closest to our RAG wellness coach concept. Proves that instructor-specific AI knowledge is valued.

**Gap:** Single instructor's knowledge, no marketplace model, no SaaS.

### Zoga Wellness (Bengaluru)
**What they do:** AI-powered yoga and meditation platform. Blended learning. Funded startup. 11 tracked competitors on Tracxn.

**Strength:** India-based, funded, AI-native.

**Gap:** Limited public feature details. Appears early-stage.

## Camp 2: Instructor / Studio SaaS

### Momoyoga (Global)
**What they do:** Market leader for yoga studio management. 1.5M+ users worldwide. Class scheduling, events, bookings, payments. Yoga-dedicated. Starting at $32/mo.

**Strength:** Massive adoption, affordable, yoga-specific. Proves yoga studios will pay for dedicated tools.

**Gap:** Zero AI anywhere. No personalization, no content generation, no intelligent onboarding.

**What to steal:** Market validation. Our instructor SaaS pricing (₹999-2,999/mo) is in range. Beat them on AI, not admin features.

### SutraSuite (Houston, USA)
**What they do:** All-in-one for wellness professionals. Booking, payments, email marketing, CRM, AI website builder, AI assistant, student portal, digital product sales.

**Strength:** One of the few integrating AI into studio management. AI website builder is clever.

**Gap:** Generic "wellness" tool, not yoga-native. No student-side AI personalization.

**What to steal:** AI website builder concept. When an instructor onboards, AI generates not just a profile but a full mini-website.

**Threat level: Low.** Closest competitor on the instructor side, but generic positioning limits it.

### YogiCircle (India)
**What they do:** Built for Indian yoga teachers. 0% commission. Branded student app, UPI + global payments, scheduling, posters, reminders.

**Strength:** India-first, teacher-friendly, affordable.

**Gap:** No AI whatsoever.

**What to steal:** India-first DNA. UPI payments native, 0% commission on direct bookings, commission only on marketplace-driven students.

### StudioYoga (Launching May 2026, Claude-powered)
**What they do:** AI-native studio management. Scheduling, communication, bookings with AI automation. Founding member price $79/mo.

**Strength:** AI-first from day one, modern stack.

**Gap:** Admin automation only. No student-facing AI, no assessment, no instructor empowerment beyond admin.

### Sanga (Tel Aviv)
**What they do:** Community-first OS for independent yoga studios. Smart booking, donation-first payments, teacher payroll. $349/mo.

**Strength:** Unique community angle. Donation-first payments caters to a real yoga studio model.

**Gap:** Expensive, no AI, Western-focused.

### Conscious Flow (Norway, 1 person)
**What they do:** "3 audiences, 1 system" — students, teachers, studios. Personalized yoga, churn prediction, revenue growth.

**Strength:** Conceptually the closest to YogaVerse AI's vision. Validates our three-audience thesis.

**Gap:** Solo founder, very early stage. Not a real competitive threat yet.

## Closest threats

**Student side: Buba** — Adaptive AI, real-time session refinement, launching 2026. Smartest consumer product in the space. Medium risk.

**Instructor side: SutraSuite** — AI-powered admin for wellness pros. Generic wellness positioning limits it. Low risk.

Neither is trying to combine both sides. We are.
